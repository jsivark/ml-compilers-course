05Calculus and gradients

Autodiff is the chain rule applied mechanically. Learn it by hand on scalars first, then for vectors and matrices, because your compiler will generate backward passes.

about 25–40 h · difficulty ●●●○○

After this module you can

  • After this module you can derive gradients of vector and matrix expressions with correct shapes, including matmul, softmax and cross-entropy.
  • After this module you can explain forward vs reverse mode autodiff and when each is cheaper.
  • After this module you can verify any gradient with finite differences and diagnose a wrong one.
  • After this module you can explain why the gradient of a broadcast is a sum and of a sum is a broadcast.

Steps

Work through these in order. Each step points at one specific unit.

  1. Watch

    3Blue1Brown Essence of Calculus ch. 1-2: The essence of calculus; The paradox of the derivative

    Then the derivatives lesson.

  2. Watch

    3Blue1Brown ch. 3-4: Derivative formulas through geometry; Visualizing the chain rule and product rule

    Also derivatives-power-rule. The chain rule lesson is the one that matters.

  3. Read

    Karpathy, Hacker's guide to Neural Networks, Chapter 1: Real-valued Circuits

    Code every gate (add, multiply, sigmoid) with forward/backward in Python; verify with numerical gradients.

  4. Read

    Parr & Howard, The Matrix Calculus You Need for Deep Learning: sections 1-4 (intro, review of scalar rules, vector calculus and partial derivatives, matrix calculus/Jacobians)

    Work the Jacobian examples by hand on paper.

  5. Read

    Parr & Howard: element-wise binary ops, scalar expansion, vector sum reduction, chain rules

    The sum-reduction and broadcasting gradients are exactly what your autograd needs for expand/sum.

  6. Read

    Parr & Howard: the gradient of neuron activation, and of the loss

    Derive dL/dW for a single linear+ReLU layer and check it numerically.

  7. Do

    Sasha Rush, Autodiff Puzzles: all puzzles

    Open the Colab notebook; derive each tensor function's gradient; the notebook checks you.

  8. Do

    Gradient check harness

    Write a finite-difference gradcheck(f, x) in NumPy and use it to verify your matmul, softmax and log-softmax derivations.

Build

Finish all Autodiff Puzzles. Then a NumPy file deriving and implementing forward/backward for matmul, sum(axis), broadcast add, ReLU, softmax and cross-entropy, each verified by your gradcheck to 1e-6 relative error.

Portfolio project: Forward-mode vs reverse-mode autodiff: implement both and measure

Implement forward-mode AD with dual numbers and reverse-mode AD on a tape, over the same small expression language (scalar ops and NumPy arrays). Construct functions with n inputs and m outputs and measure time and memory as n and m vary, demonstrating the n-vs-m crossover; then compute a Hessian-vector product by composing the two (forward-over-reverse).

Deliverables

  • repo with both AD modes, a shared gradcheck, and tests against PyTorch or JAX
  • README with plots of runtime and memory vs n and m for both modes, and the HVP verified against finite differences
  • a 600–1200 word write-up explaining JVPs, VJPs, why deep learning uses reverse mode, and what the tape costs in memory

Rubric

Solid
Both modes agree with PyTorch to 1e-8 in float64 on at least 10 test functions; crossover demonstrated in a plot.
Exceptional
Implements HVP and uses it in a few steps of Newton-CG on a small problem, adds gradient checkpointing to the tape and plots the memory/compute trade-off, and relates the design to JAX's jvp/vjp.

References: Baydin et al., Automatic Differentiation in Machine Learning: a Survey

If you get stuck

  • Shape errors in matrix calculus: always write shapes next to every term; dL/dA must have A's shape, which fixes where the transposes go.
  • Gradcheck failing at 1e-3: use float64, central differences, eps around 1e-6, and relative error; float32 will not reach 1e-6.
  • Softmax overflow: subtract the max before exp; the gradient is unchanged (Parr & Howard do not cover this, but every implementation does it).
  • ReLU gradcheck fails near 0 because the function is not differentiable there; avoid inputs within eps of the kink.

Ready to move on when

  • Derive d/dx of sigmoid and express it in terms of sigmoid itself.
  • For C = A @ B, give dL/dA and dL/dB in terms of dL/dC, with shapes.
  • Explain why the gradient of a broadcast is a sum over the broadcast axes.
  • Explain why softmax+cross-entropy has the simple gradient p - y.
  • All autodiff puzzles pass.
