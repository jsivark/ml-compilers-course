About

A free, independent rebuild of CMU 10-411 Compilers for Machine Learning, with the background a beginner needs to take it on. It isn't affiliated with CMU.

It follows the course's public topic list and Week 1 handout. It was put together with AI assistance and reviewed by a person working through it. Links are checked weekly.
