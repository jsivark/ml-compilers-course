About this course

How the course was made, what has been checked, what hasn't, and how to fix it when it's wrong.

What it is

An independent, free rebuild of CMU 10-411 Compilers for Machine Learning (Spring 2027, George Hotz). The real course's lectures and tests aren't public. This course follows its public topic list, its Week 1 handout and its technical syllabus, and adds the background a beginner needs before starting it. It is not affiliated with CMU.

How it was made

The curriculum was put together with heavy help from an AI assistant (Claude), directed and reviewed by a person who is working through it. We say so plainly because unreviewed AI output is a real problem, and you deserve to know what you're relying on. Here is what we did about it:

  • Every link is checked by a script (tools/check_content.py --links), and the check runs automatically every week. A few sites refuse automated checkers; those are marked and checked by hand.
  • Every module was reviewed against its sources. Chapter numbers, lecture titles, problem names and section references were compared with the actual pages. Claims that couldn't be confirmed were rewritten or removed.
  • The Module 18 tests were checked against a reference implementation written for that purpose. All 65 expected outcomes agree with it.
  • A style check rejects filler. It fails on boilerplate phrases and on lines repeated across modules.

Known limitations

  • The tests for Modules 19–24 are starter sets. Their expected answers were worked out by hand, and some depend on rules this course chose where the real course hasn't published a spec (marked (convention) on the assignments page). They will grow and be checked against reference implementations over time.
  • Effort estimates are estimates. They're based on published lab and lecture lengths where those exist. Your pace will differ.
  • Links rot. The weekly check catches dead links, but a page can move while its URL still works.
  • Some modules need hardware you may not own. GPU modules need an NVIDIA GPU, and Module 33 needs a Hopper-class GPU (rentable by the hour).

Found a mistake?

Please open an issue. Include the module number, the step, and what's wrong. Pull requests are welcome. Content lives in curriculum/*.json, and python3 site/build.py rebuilds the site.
