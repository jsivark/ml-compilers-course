Final projects

Modules 29–31. Pick one, build a baseline and a benchmark harness, improve it, and write it up as a 4-page report. See Module 29 for the process.

  • Equality saturation for tensor graphs. Replace greedy rewriting with an e-graph and measure the speedup. References: egg, Tensat.
  • Kernel search. BEAM or learned-cost autotuning of tile sizes and axis orders. References: tinygrad BEAM notes, Ansor.
  • Flash attention backward pass, generated by your compiler and checked against finite differences. References: FlashAttention-2, flash-attention-minimal.
  • A new backend: Metal, WebGPU/WGSL, AMD HIP, or a microcontroller. Reference: WGSL spec.
  • Quantized LLM serving: int4 weights, paged KV cache and speculative decoding, benchmarked against gpt-fast. References: gpt-fast, nano-vllm.
  • Train GPT-2 (124M) with your compiler, matching llm.c's loss curve. Reference: llm.c GPT-2 reproduction.
  • Automatic sharding: a pass that picks data or tensor parallelism per layer from a cost model. Reference: How to Scale Your Model.
  • Implement a paper. Any ML-compiler paper from awesome-tensor-compilers.
