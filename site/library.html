Library

Things to keep nearby: videos for fun, maps of the wider field, blogs to follow, and books worth borrowing.

Watch for fun

  • 3Blue1Brown: math you can see.
  • Ben Eater: an 8-bit computer on breadboards.
  • Branch Education: how chips and GPUs are physically built.
  • Welch Labs: deep learning, visually.
  • Computerphile: short explainers on compilers, floats and memory.
  • Asianometry: the history of chips and accelerators.
  • GPU MODE: kernel engineers explaining their work.

Maps of the territory

  • Teach Yourself CS and OSSU: the broader CS education this course sits inside.
  • build-your-own-x: more from-scratch projects for when you want a detour.
  • Machine Learning Systems (Harvard CS249r): an open textbook.
  • Hazy Research, AI systems building blocks, and the GPU MODE resource stream.
  • Blogs to follow: Horace He, Simon Boehm, Lei Mao, Lilian Weng, Colfax Research, Hazy Research, Jeremy Kun.

Worth buying or borrowing

  • Bryant & O'Hallaron, Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective, the textbook for CMU 15-213.
  • Hwu, Kirk & El Hajj, Programming Massively Parallel Processors, 4th ed.
  • Nora Sandler, Writing a C Compiler.
  • Hennessy & Patterson, Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach.
